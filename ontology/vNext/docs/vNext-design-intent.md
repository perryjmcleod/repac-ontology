# REPAC vNext — Design Intent

## 1. Purpose of vNext

REPAC vNext exists to extend REPAC v1.0 without altering its semantics, authority, or scope. vNext introduces optional analytical resolution, formal rigor enhancements, mapping layers, and application artifacts for contexts where the flat v1.0 Superset model requires additional explanatory detail.

## 2. What v1.0 intentionally does not do

REPAC v1.0 intentionally avoids internal decomposition (no Subsets/Elements), workflows, sequences, maturity models, domain assumptions, and context reification. These exclusions preserve domain independence, auditability, and a non-prescriptive stance.

## 3. Analytical pressure motivating vNext

vNext responds to repeated analytical pressure where Supersets remain necessary but lack sufficient resolution for certain analyses, teaching, tooling, or interoperability requirements.

## 4. What vNext may add

### 4.1 Subsets and Elements (normative extensions)
Optional decompositions under Supersets where resolution is demonstrably required. Supersets remain the only required analytical commitments.

### 4.2 Formalization enhancements
Additional OWL axioms and SHACL shapes that improve constraint clarity and reasoning behavior without semantic drift.

### 4.3 Mapping and alignment layers (non-normative)
Mappings to standards, methods, domain models, and optional foundational alignments. Mappings are translation aids and do not assert equivalence or dependency.

### 4.4 Applications and pedagogy (non-normative)
Worked cases, teaching views, practitioner documentation, and tooling concepts. These demonstrate use; they do not define semantics.

## 5. What vNext will never add

No reinterpretation of v1.0 semantics, no renaming/redefining Supersets, no collapsing Supersets, no workflows/stages/maturity models, no context reification, and no changes that make any Superset optional or bypassable.

## 6. Normativity and authority rules

REPAC v1.0 remains canonical. vNext is cited as an extension of v1.0. Normative vs non-normative status must be explicit per module.

## 7. Criteria for success

vNext adds resolution without prescription, preserves Superset necessity, improves explanatory clarity in real cases, supports teaching and application, and remains modular and reviewable.

## 8. Lock statement

This document defines the design-intent boundary for REPAC vNext. Changes require explicit revision and review.
