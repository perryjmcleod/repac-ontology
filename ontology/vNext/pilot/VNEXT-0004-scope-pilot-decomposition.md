# VNEXT-0004 — Scope Pilot Decomposition (Controlled)

## 1. Superset
Scope

## 2. Objective
Introduce a controlled Subset/Element decomposition for Scope under VNEXT-0001 and VNEXT-0002, without introducing prescription, workflow, stages, or maturity implications.

## 3. Pilot Constraints
- Four Subsets maximum
- Three Elements maximum per Subset
- No cross-Superset reuse during pilot
- No domain qualifiers
- No examples embedded in definitions

## 4. Pilot Subsets
- VNEXT-SCOPE-S1 — Inclusion Commitments
- VNEXT-SCOPE-S2 — Exclusion Commitments
- VNEXT-SCOPE-S3 — Assumption and Fixity Commitments
- VNEXT-SCOPE-S4 — Deferral Commitments

## 5. Governance
All constructs in this pilot must satisfy VNEXT-0001 eligibility criteria and follow VNEXT-0002 templates. Any proposed construct failing eligibility must be recorded in the reject log.
