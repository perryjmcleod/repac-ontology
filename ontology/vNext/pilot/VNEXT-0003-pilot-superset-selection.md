# VNEXT-0003 — Pilot Superset Selection

## Selected Superset
Scope

## Justification
Scope is selected for controlled pilot decomposition because:

1. High analytical load with low prescription risk  
2. Clear boundary semantics (inclusion, exclusion, assumption, deferral)  
3. Clean interaction with other Supersets without overlap  
4. Least risk of method or lifecycle misinterpretation

## Pilot Constraints
- Maximum 3–4 Subsets
- Maximum 3 Elements per Subset
- No cross-Superset reuse
- No domain qualifiers
- No examples embedded in definitions

## Explicit Non-Goals
This pilot does not:
- Establish a preferred Scope model
- Imply completeness
- Generalize decomposition rules
- Introduce practices or tooling
