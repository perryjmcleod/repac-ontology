# REPAC vNext — Phase V-6 Governance Decision
## Second Superset Pilot Selection

**Decision ID:** VNEXT-0008  
**Phase:** V-6 — Second Superset Controlled Pilot  
**Status:** Approved  
**Applies To:** REPAC vNext only  
**Date:** 2026-02-08  

### Decision
The **Governance Superset** is selected as the **second Superset** for controlled pilot decomposition in REPAC vNext.

### Rationale
1. **Boundary Symmetry with Scope**: Scope constrains what is included, excluded, fixed, or deferred. Governance constrains who may decide, authorize, override, or enforce. Both operate as structural constraint layers.
2. **High Explanatory Leverage, Low Procedural Gravity**: Governance supports explanation without implying sequencing, maturity, or organizational design.
3. **Comparative Audit Value**: Sequencing Governance after Scope supports direct comparison between boundary commitments and authority constraints.
4. **Risk Containment**: Governance can be decomposed as analytical constraint commitments without introducing governance models, control frameworks, or compliance checklists.

### Explicit Non-Goals
This pilot does not:
- Introduce governance models, frameworks, or standards
- Define roles, committees, workflows, or approval processes
- Prescribe control mechanisms, compliance practices, or oversight maturity
- Reinterpret or extend REPAC v1.0 semantics

### Execution Constraints
- Markdown-only artifacts
- Script-first generation
- No diagrams, OWL, TTL, or SHACL during pilot
- Necessary-but-not-sufficient framing preserved
- No cross-Superset refactoring beyond comparative notes

### Expected Learning Objectives
- Governance decomposition without procedural bleed-through
- Scope–Governance constraint pairing evaluation
- Depth without increased prescription risk

### Governance Record
This decision is authoritative for REPAC vNext Phase V-6 and does not alter or reinterpret REPAC v1.0.
