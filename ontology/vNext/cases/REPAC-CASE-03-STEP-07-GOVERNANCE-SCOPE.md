## Purpose of This Artifact

This document surfaces how **delivery governance commitments constrained scope definitions**, and how **scope choices shaped interpretations of governance success**, without asserting mismanagement or error.

---

## Governance → Scope Constraint Pathways

### G→S.1 Delivery Commitments as Scope Anchors
- Governance emphasized:
  - Schedule adherence
  - Budget compliance
- These commitments stabilized scope around deliverables rather than outcomes.

### G→S.2 Change Control and Scope Rigidity
- Formal change controls:
  - Discouraged late scope adjustments
  - Prioritized predictability over responsiveness
- Governance mechanisms favored scope freeze once delivery milestones were set.

### G→S.3 Reporting Structures
- Governance reporting focused on:
  - Milestone completion
  - Artifact readiness
- Outcome-oriented scope elements were less visible.

---

## Scope → Governance Stressors

### S→G.1 Outcome Ambiguity
- Narrowly defined scope limited governance visibility into adoption and value.
- Governance success signals became decoupled from user experience.

### S→G.2 Post-Delivery Reinterpretation
- Once delivery succeeded, governance narratives faced pressure to explain perceived failure.
- Scope boundaries constrained available explanations.
