# REPAC Case 02 — Step 08: Communication and Collaboration Analysis
## Communication–Collaboration Cross-Domain Constraint Mapping

## Case Reference
- **Case ID:** REPAC-CASE-02**
- **Case Title:** Compliance-Driven Training That Reduces Performance
- **Domains in Focus:** Communication ↔ Collaboration

---

## Purpose of This Artifact

This document distinguishes **communication of obligation** from **collaboration on applicability**, ensuring that performance impacts are not reduced to messaging quality alone.

---

## Communication → Collaboration Constraint Pathways

### C→Co.1 Obligation-Centric Messaging
- Communication emphasized:
  - Requirement
  - Deadline
  - Completion tracking
- This framing constrained:
  - Collaborative adaptation
  - Local contextualization

### C→Co.2 Directional Communication Flow
- Messaging flowed top-down.
- Communication channels signaled execution, not dialogue.

---

## Collaboration → Communication Stressors

### Co→C.1 Absence of Design Collaboration
- Limited collaboration during design increased:
  - Reliance on explanatory communication
  - Post-hoc justification of impacts

### Co→C.2 Feedback Bottlenecks
- Collaboration mechanisms existed but were downstream.
- Communication bore burden of addressing emergent concerns.

---

## Interaction Patterns (Non-Evaluative)

| Pattern | Structural Effect |
|-------|-------------------|
| Directive communication | Collaboration constrained |
| Late feedback | Communication load increased |
| Limited co-design | Adaptation deferred |

---

## Explicit Non-Claims

No claim is made that:
- Communication was unclear
- Collaboration was resisted
- Actors acted in bad faith
