# Step 6 — Case 01
## Domain-by-Domain Analytical Question Formulation

**Case:** REPAC-CASE-01 — Promotion Without Posting  
**Objective:** Make all explanatory commitments explicit before any diagnosis.

## Governance
1. What formal governance rules, policies, or procedures govern promotion decisions in this organization?
2. Were those rules explicitly invoked, bypassed, or reinterpreted in this case?
3. If an exception pathway exists, what conditions are required to be met to invoke it?
4. Who holds decision rights for bypassing standard posting procedures?
5. Was any deviation documented, justified, or auditable?
6. How are transparency and procedural fairness defined in the governance framework?
7. What accountability mechanisms exist when governance norms are not followed?
8. Was post-decision communication required by governance, and was it performed?

## Scope
1. What decision boundaries were declared or implied for this promotion decision?
2. Who was included and excluded from consideration, and on what basis?
3. Was the scope of eligible candidates explicitly constrained or implicitly assumed?
4. Were time, urgency, or operational constraints cited to narrow scope?
5. Were alternative options (posting, interim appointment, acting role) considered within scope?
6. Was the scope of impact (team, department, organization) assessed or acknowledged?

## Communication
1. What information about the decision process was communicated, and to whom?
2. At what point in the decision timeline did communication occur?
3. Were reasons for bypassing posting communicated proactively or reactively?
4. Was the communication framed as policy-based, exception-based, or discretionary?
5. Were affected parties given an opportunity to ask questions or seek clarification?
6. How was meaning expected to be interpreted by recipients of the communication?

## Collaboration
1. Which actors participated in or influenced the promotion decision?
2. Were HR, management, or peer stakeholders consulted or informed?
3. Were dissenting perspectives solicited or suppressed?
4. How were interdependencies between teams or roles considered?
5. Did collaboration occur before, during, or after the decision?
6. Were feedback or escalation channels available and used?

## Purpose
1. What organizational purpose was the promotion intended to serve?
2. Was that purpose explicitly stated or implicitly assumed?
3. Was speed, continuity, merit, retention, or risk mitigation prioritized?
4. Were competing purposes acknowledged or reconciled?

## Value
1. What value outcomes were anticipated from the decision?
2. For whom was value expected to be created?
3. What potential harms or trade-offs were acknowledged?
4. Were morale, trust, or legitimacy considered as value dimensions?
5. How would success or failure of the decision be evaluated?
