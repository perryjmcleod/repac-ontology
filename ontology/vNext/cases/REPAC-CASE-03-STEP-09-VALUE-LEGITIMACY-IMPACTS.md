## Value Impact Surfaces

### V.1 Delivery Value
- Predictable execution
- Budget and schedule certainty

### V.2 Adoption Value
- Inconsistent uptake
- Workflow misalignment

### V.3 Experiential Value
- User frustration
- Perceived misfit

---

## Legitimacy Surfaces

### L.1 Procedural Legitimacy
- Confidence in delivery governance

### L.2 Outcome Legitimacy
- Questions about whether “success” matched intent

### L.3 Narrative Legitimacy
- Tension between official success claims and lived experience

---

## Interaction Pathways

- Delivery value did not guarantee outcome legitimacy.
- Legitimacy concerns amplified focus on adoption gaps.
