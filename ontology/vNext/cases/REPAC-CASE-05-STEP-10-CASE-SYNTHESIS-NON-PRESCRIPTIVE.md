# REPAC Case 05 — Step 10: Case Synthesis (Non-Prescriptive)

## Purpose of This Step
This synthesis integrates observations across REPAC domains while preserving analytical separation and avoiding prescriptive claims.

## Synthesis
Case 05 demonstrates how organizations sustain delivery under constraint through adaptive behavior that is only partially represented by formal constructs. Governance mechanisms and scope definitions existed, but execution relied heavily on individual judgment, informal communication, and selective collaboration.

The case cannot be explained through a single-domain lens. Governance was present but unevenly enforced. Scope was defined but enacted flexibly. Communication supported immediate action while limiting shared understanding. Collaboration resolved local dependencies while increasing systemic fragility.

Value outcomes were achieved in the short term, but legitimacy became conditional and uneven. REPAC analysis shows that these outcomes arise from the interaction of multiple domains rather than isolated failure, reinforcing the need for disciplined, multi-domain explanation.
