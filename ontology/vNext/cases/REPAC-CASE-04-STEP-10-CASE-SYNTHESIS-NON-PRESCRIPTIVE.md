# REPAC Case 04 — Step 10: Case Synthesis (Non-Prescriptive)

## Purpose of This Step
This synthesis integrates observations across domains while preserving REPAC’s non-prescriptive analytical stance.

## Synthesis
This case demonstrates that shadow processes adopted by high performers are not inherently failures of governance or discipline. They often arise from misalignment between formal constructs and lived operational reality.

The shadow process functioned as an adaptive response to unmet imperatives and constrained scope, delivering value where formal mechanisms could not. At the same time, the mechanisms that enabled high performance introduced fragility, opacity, and legitimacy erosion.

REPAC analysis shows that the phenomenon cannot be reduced to a single-domain explanation. Purpose alignment coexisted with governance weakness; value creation coexisted with legitimacy risk. Maintaining analytical separation across domains preserves explanatory fidelity and avoids simplistic diagnosis.
