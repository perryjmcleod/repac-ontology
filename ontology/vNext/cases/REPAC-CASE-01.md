# REPAC Case 01 — Promotion Without Posting

- Case Type: Organizational decision process anomaly
- Decision Class: Staffing / advancement
- Temporal Scope: Single decision event with downstream effects

## Primary Domains Implicated (Non-Exclusive)
- Governance
- Scope
- Communication
- Collaboration

## Problem Statement (Bounded)
A promotion was awarded to an individual without a formal posting or open competition, despite the presence of established organizational governance norms that typically require posting and transparent selection processes.

The issue under analysis is not whether the selected individual was qualified or deserving.
The issue is whether governance commitments related to transparency, procedural fairness, and decision justification were followed, bypassed, or reinterpreted.

## Salient Facts (Observable)
1. A promotion opportunity existed.
2. The position was not posted internally or externally.
3. The role was awarded directly to a specific individual.
4. The selected individual has fewer years of experience than at least one other potential candidate.
5. At least one other employee expressed interest in advancement opportunities.
6. Organizational norms exist regarding posting roles prior to promotion.
7. No formal explanation was initially provided for bypassing the posting process.
8. The affected party learned of the decision after the promotion was awarded.
9. The concern raised relates to governance process, not personal entitlement.

## Explicit Exclusions and Unknowns
- Formal job description of the role
- Existence or invocation of exception policies
- Performance evaluations of candidates
- Legal or collective agreement constraints
- Intent or motivation of decision-makers
- Whether deviation constituted policy violation or accepted exception
- Historical precedent for similar decisions
