# REPAC Case 07 — Step 10: Case Synthesis (Non-Prescriptive)

## Purpose of This Step
This synthesis integrates observations across REPAC domains while maintaining analytical separation and avoiding prescriptive conclusions.

## Synthesis
Case 07 illustrates how organizations sustain short-term functionality through adaptive behavior that is only partially represented by formal constructs. Governance mechanisms and scope definitions provided structural reference points, but execution depended heavily on selective communication, informal collaboration, and individual judgment.

The case cannot be explained by a single-domain deficiency. Governance mechanisms existed but were applied episodically. Scope was defined but enacted flexibly. Communication enabled immediate action while limiting shared understanding. Collaboration resolved dependencies locally while increasing systemic fragility.

Value outcomes were achieved unevenly, with success dependent on informal networks and tacit knowledge. At the same time, legitimacy became conditional, weakening the authority of formal governance and increasing reliance on situational judgment.

REPAC analysis demonstrates that these outcomes emerge from the interaction of multiple domains rather than from isolated failure. The case reinforces the importance of multi-domain explanation when examining organizational performance under coordination and dependency constraints.
