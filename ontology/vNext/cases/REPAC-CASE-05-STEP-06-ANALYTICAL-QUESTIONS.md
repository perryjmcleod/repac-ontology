# REPAC Case 05 — Step 06: Analytical Questions

## Purpose of This Step
This step defines the analytical questions required to explain the conditions observed in Case 05. The intent is to ensure that explanation remains distributed across REPAC domains and does not collapse into a single-factor narrative such as governance failure, resource constraint, or individual behavior.

## Analytical Questions by Domain

### Purpose
- What organizational purpose(s) were formally associated with the work in this case?
- How did participants interpret or prioritize purpose under operational pressure?

### Imperative
- What imperatives (time pressure, compliance demands, risk tolerance, workload, dependency constraints) shaped behavior?
- Were these imperatives explicit, implicit, or emergent during execution?

### Scope
- How was scope formally defined in terms of responsibilities, boundaries, and deliverables?
- Where did execution require expansion, contraction, or reinterpretation of scope?

### Work
- What work was expected to occur according to formal descriptions or plans?
- What work was actually performed, deferred, bypassed, or substituted?

### Governance
- What governance mechanisms were intended to guide behavior and decision-making?
- How consistently were governance expectations enforced in practice?

### Communication
- How were expectations, assumptions, and changes communicated across participants?
- Where did communication rely on inference or informal understanding rather than explicit articulation?

### Collaboration
- Who was expected to collaborate formally, and who collaborated in practice?
- How were cross-boundary dependencies resolved when formal coordination proved insufficient?

### Value
- What value outcomes were anticipated by the organization?
- What value was realized, delayed, displaced, or placed at risk as a result of the case conditions?
