# REPAC Case 05 — Step 09: Value and Legitimacy Impacts

## Purpose of This Step
This step documents value impacts and legitimacy impacts associated with Case 05 without asserting prescriptive conclusions.

## Value Impacts

### Positive Value Signals
- Progress toward visible deliverables under constraint.
- Reduced friction for experienced participants navigating informal pathways.

### Negative Value Signals
- Increased rework due to misaligned assumptions.
- Uneven performance outcomes dependent on individual capability.
- Deferred quality, documentation, or validation work.

### Hidden and Deferred Costs
- Accumulation of coordination and procedural debt.
- Increased reliance on tacit knowledge and key individuals.

## Legitimacy Impacts
Legitimacy was conditional and outcome-driven:
- Deviations from formal expectations were tolerated when results were achieved.
- Governance credibility weakened as rule enforcement appeared situational.
- Informal norms gained authority relative to formal structures.
