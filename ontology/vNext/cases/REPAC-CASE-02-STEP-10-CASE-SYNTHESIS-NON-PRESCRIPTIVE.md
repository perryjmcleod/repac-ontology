# REPAC Case 02 — Step 10: Case Synthesis (Non-Prescriptive)
## Case Synthesis (Non-Prescriptive)

This case demonstrates how **compliance-oriented governance**, when combined with **broad, uniform scope and limited collaboration**, can satisfy formal mandates while producing contested value outcomes.

The case does not show training failure.  
It shows **explanatory insufficiency when compliance is treated as a proxy for value**.

---

## Analytical Pattern (Descriptive)

> When governance success is defined narrowly by completion, value interpretation shifts to operational and relational domains.

---

## What This Case Is Not

- Not a critique of compliance
- Not an argument against mandatory training
- Not evidence of mismanagement

---

## What This Case Demonstrates

- The separation of compliance from performance
- The role of scope in shaping perceived value
- The importance of collaboration beyond communication
