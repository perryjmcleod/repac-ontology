# REPAC Case 04 — Step 08: Communication and Collaboration Analysis

## Purpose of This Step
This step examines how communication and collaboration patterns enabled the shadow process to emerge, stabilize, and propagate.

## Communication Analysis
Communication surrounding the shadow process was tacit and experiential rather than explicit and procedural. Practices were demonstrated, observed, and socially reinforced rather than documented. This limited visibility to formal oversight while accelerating adoption among peers.

Information sharing emphasized operational effectiveness and practical shortcuts, with little emphasis on compliance or traceability. As a result, awareness of the shadow process remained uneven across the organization.

## Collaboration Analysis
Collaboration shifted from role-based coordination to relationship-based coordination. Participants were selected based on perceived competence and reliability rather than formal assignment.

This reduced coordination overhead and increased execution speed but introduced fragility. Participation depended on individual availability and informal trust rather than organizational structure.

## Integrated Observation
Together, informal communication and selective collaboration created a high-performance micro-system that operated parallel to, rather than integrated with, formal organizational mechanisms.
