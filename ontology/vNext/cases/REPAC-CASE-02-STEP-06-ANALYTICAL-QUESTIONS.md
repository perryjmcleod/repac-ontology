# Step 6 — Case 02
## Domain-by-Domain Analytical Question Formulation

**Case:** REPAC-CASE-02 — Compliance-Driven Training That Reduces Performance  
**Objective:** Make all explanatory commitments explicit before any diagnosis.

## Purpose
1. What organizational purpose was the training intended to serve?
2. Was compliance the primary or secondary purpose?
3. Were performance outcomes explicitly part of the stated purpose?

## Governance
1. What mandates or regulatory requirements drove the training?
2. How was compliance success defined?
3. Were performance risks acknowledged within governance artifacts?

## Scope
1. Who was required to complete the training?
2. Were contextual differences among participants considered?
3. Was scope uniform by design or by default?

## Work
1. How did the training alter day-to-day work practices?
2. Did training requirements displace productive work?
3. Were adaptations permitted?

## Value
1. What value outcomes were anticipated?
2. For whom was value expected to be realized?
3. How was value measured beyond compliance completion?

## Communication
1. How was the purpose of the training communicated?
2. Were trade-offs disclosed?

## Collaboration
1. Were frontline stakeholders consulted in design or rollout?
2. Were feedback mechanisms available during execution?
