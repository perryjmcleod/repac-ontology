## Purpose of This Artifact

This document surfaces how **compliance-driven governance mechanisms constrain training scope**, and how **scope decisions amplify governance pressure**, without asserting that either was inappropriate.

The goal is to make **constraint interactions explicit** so performance outcomes are not misattributed to training quality alone.

---

## Governance → Scope Constraint Pathways

### G→S.1 Mandated Participation as Scope Definition
- Governance requirements defined training as mandatory.
- Mandatory status:
  - Removed discretion over participation
  - Expanded scope to include heterogeneous roles
- Governance emphasis on uniform compliance constrained tailoring.

### G→S.2 Auditability and Scope Uniformity
- Governance requirements for auditability favored:
  - Standardized content
  - Consistent completion evidence
- This constrained scope flexibility even where work contexts differed.

### G→S.3 Exception Risk
- Exceptions to scope risked:
  - Perceived non-compliance
  - Increased governance scrutiny
- Scope breadth functioned as a compliance safeguard.

---

## Scope → Governance Stressors

### S→G.1 Broad Scope Oversight Load
- Broad participant scope increased:
  - Governance monitoring burden
  - Reporting and tracking complexity
- Governance systems absorbed scope-induced overhead.

### S→G.2 Scope–Value Tension
- Uniform scope obscured differential value realization.
- Governance success metrics focused on completion, not outcomes.
