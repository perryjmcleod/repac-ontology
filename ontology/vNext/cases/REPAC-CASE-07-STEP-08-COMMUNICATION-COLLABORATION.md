# REPAC Case 07 — Step 08: Communication and Collaboration Analysis

## Purpose of This Step
This step examines communication and collaboration patterns that shaped coordination, dependency management, and outcome realization in Case 07.

## Communication Analysis
Communication was characterized by selective transmission:
- Information critical to immediate action was prioritized over contextual completeness.
- Assumptions and rationale were frequently implicit rather than articulated.
- Formal communication channels lagged behind real-time coordination needs.

As a result, shared understanding was sufficient for local execution but insufficient for systemic alignment.

## Collaboration Analysis
Collaboration was adaptive and relationship-driven:
- Participants relied on informal networks to bridge gaps between formal roles.
- Collaboration effectiveness depended heavily on prior working relationships and individual credibility.
- Formal coordination structures were bypassed when they introduced delay or ambiguity.

## Integrated Observation
The interaction of selective communication and relationship-based collaboration enabled short-term coordination while increasing the risk of misalignment, knowledge fragmentation, and uneven performance across the broader system.
