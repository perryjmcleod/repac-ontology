# REPAC Case 04 — Step 06: Analytical Questions

## Purpose of This Step
This step surfaces the core explanatory questions required to account for the emergence and persistence of a shadow process adopted by high performers. The intent is not to judge the legitimacy of the shadow process itself, but to discipline inquiry across REPAC domains so that explanation does not collapse into single-factor narratives (e.g., “governance failure” or “rogue behavior”).

## Analytical Questions by Domain

### Purpose
- What organizational purpose(s) were actors attempting to satisfy that were insufficiently supported by the formal process?
- Were high performers aligned with stated organizational objectives, or were they optimizing for locally inferred outcomes?

### Imperative
- What constraints (time, risk, workload, performance pressure) rendered the formal process impractical or insufficient in practice?
- Were imperatives explicit (e.g., deadlines, service levels) or tacit (e.g., reputational expectations, peer norms)?

### Scope
- What activities, decisions, or responsibilities fell outside the effective operational scope of the formal process?
- Where did the shadow process expand, contract, or bypass defined scope boundaries?

### Work
- What work was actually performed differently in the shadow process compared to the formal process?
- Which tasks were omitted, reordered, accelerated, or combined?

### Governance
- What governance mechanisms existed formally, and where did they fail to constrain or guide actual behavior?
- Were exceptions visible, tolerated, or implicitly encouraged?

### Communication
- How was knowledge about the shadow process shared among high performers?
- What information about the shadow process was withheld, downplayed, or normalized?

### Collaboration
- Who participated in the shadow process, and how were participants implicitly selected?
- Did collaboration patterns differ materially from those defined in formal structures?

### Value
- What value outcomes improved as a result of the shadow process?
- What risks, trade-offs, or downstream impacts were created but not immediately visible?
