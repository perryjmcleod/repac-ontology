# REPAC Case 06 — Step 06: Analytical Questions

## Purpose of This Step
This step establishes the minimum analytical questions required to explain the conditions observed in Case 06. The objective is to prevent premature causal attribution and to ensure that explanation remains distributed across REPAC domains rather than collapsing into a single-domain diagnosis.

## Analytical Questions by Domain

### Purpose
- What organizational purpose(s) were formally articulated for the work in question?
- How did participants interpret or prioritize purpose under operational conditions?

### Imperative
- What constraints (time, compliance pressure, risk exposure, workload, external dependency) shaped decision-making?
- Were imperatives explicitly stated, implicitly inferred, or retrospectively justified?

### Scope
- How was scope formally defined for the work?
- Where did actual execution require expansion, contraction, or reinterpretation of scope boundaries?

### Work
- What work was expected to occur according to formal descriptions?
- What work was actually performed, deferred, bypassed, or substituted?

### Governance
- What governance mechanisms were intended to guide or constrain the work?
- How consistently were governance expectations enforced or audited?

### Communication
- How was information about expectations, constraints, and progress communicated?
- Where did communication breakdowns, compression, or ambiguity emerge?

### Collaboration
- Who was required to collaborate formally, and who collaborated in practice?
- How were dependencies managed when formal coordination proved insufficient?

### Value
- What value outcomes were anticipated?
- What value was actually realized, deferred, or displaced as a result of the case conditions?
