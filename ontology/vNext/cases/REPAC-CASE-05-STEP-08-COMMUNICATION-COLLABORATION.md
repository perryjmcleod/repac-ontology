# REPAC Case 05 — Step 08: Communication and Collaboration Analysis

## Purpose of This Step
This step examines how communication and collaboration patterns influenced coordination, decision-making, and execution in Case 05.

## Communication Analysis
Communication was shaped by urgency and prioritization:
- Information critical to immediate action was emphasized.
- Context, rationale, and downstream implications were frequently compressed or omitted.
- Formal communication channels lagged behind operational needs.

This produced partial shared understanding rather than a fully aligned operating picture.

## Collaboration Analysis
Collaboration was adaptive and selective:
- Participants relied on informal networks to resolve dependencies.
- Collaboration effectiveness depended on prior relationships and individual credibility.
- Formal coordination mechanisms were bypassed when they introduced delay.

## Integrated Observation
Communication and collaboration enabled short-term coordination while increasing the risk of misalignment, knowledge fragmentation, and uneven execution across the system.
