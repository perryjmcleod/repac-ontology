# REPAC Case 04 — Step 09: Value and Legitimacy Impacts

## Purpose of This Step
This step evaluates impacts on value creation and organizational legitimacy without prescribing corrective action.

## Value Impacts

### Positive
- Increased throughput and reduced cycle time
- Improved outcome quality and reliability
- Enhanced responsiveness to operational demands

### Neutral
- Minimal immediate cost impact due to reliance on existing personnel and tools

### Negative
- Reduced transparency and auditability
- Uneven distribution of capability and knowledge
- Accumulation of hidden technical or procedural debt

## Legitimacy Impacts
The shadow process occupied an ambiguous legitimacy position. It was tacitly accepted due to performance benefits but remained formally illegitimate due to non-compliance with documented procedures.

This duality weakened governance credibility. Rules were known but selectively ignored, signaling that compliance was conditional on performance outcomes rather than organizational principle.
