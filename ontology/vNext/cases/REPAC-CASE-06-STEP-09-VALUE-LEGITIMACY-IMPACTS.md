# REPAC Case 06 — Step 09: Value and Legitimacy Impacts

## Purpose of This Step
This step documents value and legitimacy impacts associated with Case 06 without asserting prescriptive conclusions.

## Value Impacts

### Positive Value Signals
- Short-term progress toward visible deliverables.
- Reduced friction for experienced participants navigating constraints.

### Negative Value Signals
- Rework resulting from misaligned assumptions and partial information.
- Increased cognitive and coordination load on a small subset of individuals.
- Deferred quality, documentation, or validation activities.

### Hidden Costs
- Accumulation of technical, procedural, or relational debt.
- Reduced resilience when key individuals were unavailable.

## Legitimacy Impacts
Legitimacy was unevenly distributed:
- Actions aligned with outcomes were retrospectively legitimized.
- Deviations from formal expectations were tolerated when they avoided immediate failure.
- Governance credibility weakened as rules appeared conditionally enforceable.
