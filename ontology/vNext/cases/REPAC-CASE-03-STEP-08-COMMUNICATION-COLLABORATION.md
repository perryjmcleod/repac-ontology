# REPAC Case 03 — Step 8
## Communication–Collaboration Cross-Domain Constraint Mapping

## Case Reference
- **Case ID:** REPAC-CASE-03**
- **Case Title:** Digital Transformation “Failure” With On-Time Delivery
- **Domains in Focus:** Communication ↔ Collaboration

---

## Purpose of This Artifact

This document separates **communication of delivery success** from **collaboration on adoption and use**, ensuring outcome dissatisfaction is not misattributed to messaging alone.

---

## Communication → Collaboration Constraint Pathways

### C→Co.1 Success-Framed Communication
- Communication emphasized:
  - On-time delivery
  - Feature completion
- This framing constrained collaborative discussion of lived experience.

### C→Co.2 Post-Delivery Messaging
- Communication occurred largely after delivery.
- Collaboration opportunities were shifted to remediation rather than co-creation.

---

## Collaboration → Communication Stressors

### Co→C.1 Limited Adoption Collaboration
- Collaboration during rollout was constrained.
- Communication absorbed responsibility for explaining outcome gaps.

### Co→C.2 Feedback Translation
- User feedback required interpretation through formal channels.
- Communication acted as proxy for unresolved collaboration.

---

## Interaction Patterns (Non-Evaluative)

| Pattern | Structural Effect |
|-------|-------------------|
| Delivery-centric messaging | Collaboration narrowed |
| Late engagement | Communication burden increased |
| Feedback mediation | Meaning distortion risk |

---

## Explicit Non-Claims

No claim is made that:
- Communication was misleading
- Collaboration was intentionally restricted
