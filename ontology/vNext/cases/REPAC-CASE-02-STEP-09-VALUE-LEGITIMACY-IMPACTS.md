# REPAC Case 02 — Step 09: Value and Legitimacy Impacts
## Value–Legitimacy Impact Mapping

## Case Reference
- **Case ID:** REPAC-CASE-02**
- **Domains in Focus:** Value ↔ Governance (Legitimacy)

---

## Value Impact Surfaces

### V.1 Compliance Value
- Regulatory assurance
- Audit defensibility

### V.2 Operational Value
- Reduced productive time
- Workflow disruption

### V.3 Human Value
- Frustration with perceived low applicability
- Motivation effects

---

## Legitimacy Surfaces

### L.1 Procedural Legitimacy
- Training viewed as rule-compliant

### L.2 Explanatory Legitimacy
- Questions about why uniformity outweighed performance impact

### L.3 Relational Legitimacy
- Trust in training governance sensitivity

---

## Interaction Pathways

- Compliance value did not guarantee legitimacy of trade-offs.
- Legitimacy concerns amplified attention to operational costs.

---

## Explicit Non-Claims

This step does **not** assert:
- Value loss
- Governance failure
- Illegitimacy
