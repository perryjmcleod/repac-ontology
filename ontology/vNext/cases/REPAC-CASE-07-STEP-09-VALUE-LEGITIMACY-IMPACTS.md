# REPAC Case 07 — Step 09: Value and Legitimacy Impacts

## Purpose of This Step
This step documents value impacts and legitimacy impacts associated with Case 07 without asserting corrective recommendations or normative judgments.

## Value Impacts

### Positive Value Signals
- Short-term continuity of operations despite coordination challenges.
- Resolution of immediate blockers through adaptive collaboration.
- Localized achievement of outcomes for participants closest to execution.

### Negative Value Signals
- Increased rework due to misaligned assumptions and incomplete shared understanding.
- Uneven performance outcomes across teams or functions.
- Increased dependency on a small set of individuals to bridge gaps.

### Hidden and Deferred Costs
- Accumulation of coordination and knowledge debt.
- Reduced reproducibility of successful outcomes.
- Increased vulnerability to personnel changes or workload spikes.

## Legitimacy Impacts
Legitimacy in Case 07 was conditional and situational:
- Actions producing acceptable outcomes were retrospectively legitimized.
- Deviations from formal expectations were tolerated when they avoided visible failure.
- Governance credibility weakened as consistency between stated rules and enacted behavior eroded.

This conditional legitimacy signaled that compliance was subordinate to outcome preservation under constraint, reinforcing informal norms over formal structures.
