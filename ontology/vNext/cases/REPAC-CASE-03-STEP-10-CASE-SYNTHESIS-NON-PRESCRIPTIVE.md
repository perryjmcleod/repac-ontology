# REPAC Case 03 — Step 10
## Case Synthesis (Non-Prescriptive)

This case demonstrates how **delivery-oriented governance and tightly bounded scope** can produce formally successful outcomes that remain contested in terms of value and legitimacy.

The case is not about failed transformation.  
It is about **misalignment between delivery success and value interpretation**.

---

## Analytical Pattern (Descriptive)

> When governance success is measured by delivery, unresolved collaboration and adoption dynamics surface as legitimacy challenges rather than execution failures.

---

## What This Case Is Not

- Not evidence of poor project management
- Not proof of communication failure
- Not a rejection of delivery discipline

---

## What This Case Demonstrates

- The distinction between delivery and adoption
- The role of scope framing in outcome interpretation
- The limits of success narratives absent collaboration
