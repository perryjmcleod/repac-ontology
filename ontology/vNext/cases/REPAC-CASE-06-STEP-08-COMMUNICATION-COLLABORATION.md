# REPAC Case 06 — Step 08: Communication and Collaboration Analysis

## Purpose of This Step
This step examines how communication and collaboration patterns influenced coordination, decision-making, and the organization’s capacity to sustain coherent execution.

## Communication Analysis
Communication in Case 06 was shaped by compression and prioritization:
- Information was filtered to what was deemed immediately actionable.
- Context, rationale, and downstream implications were often omitted.
- Formal communication channels lagged behind real-time operational needs.

This resulted in partial shared understanding rather than a common operating picture.

## Collaboration Analysis
Collaboration was adaptive and situational:
- Participants relied on informal networks to resolve dependencies.
- Collaboration effectiveness depended on prior relationships and individual credibility.
- Formal coordination mechanisms were bypassed when they introduced delay.

## Integrated Observation
The combined effect of compressed communication and selective collaboration produced local problem-solving success while increasing the risk of misalignment across the broader system.
