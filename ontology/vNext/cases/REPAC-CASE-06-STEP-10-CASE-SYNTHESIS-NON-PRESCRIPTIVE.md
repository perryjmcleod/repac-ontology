# REPAC Case 06 — Step 10: Case Synthesis (Non-Prescriptive)

## Purpose of This Step
This synthesis integrates observations across REPAC domains while preserving analytical separation and avoiding prescriptive claims.

## Synthesis
Case 06 illustrates how organizations maintain momentum under constraint through adaptive behavior that is only partially represented by formal constructs. Governance and scope definitions provided structural reference points, but execution relied heavily on individual judgment, informal coordination, and compressed communication.

The case cannot be explained by governance weakness alone. Governance mechanisms existed but were applied unevenly. Scope was defined but enacted flexibly. Communication enabled rapid action while sacrificing shared understanding. Collaboration solved local problems while introducing systemic fragility.

Value outcomes were achieved in the short term, but at the cost of increased dependency on tacit knowledge and conditional legitimacy. REPAC analysis demonstrates that these outcomes emerge from the interaction of multiple domains rather than from a single causal deficiency.

This case reinforces the necessity of multi-domain explanation when examining organizational performance under constraint.
