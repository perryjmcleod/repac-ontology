# Step 6 — Case 03
## Domain-by-Domain Analytical Question Formulation

**Case:** REPAC-CASE-03 — Digital Transformation “Failure” With On-Time Delivery  
**Objective:** Make all explanatory commitments explicit before any diagnosis.

---

## Purpose

1. What organizational purpose was the digital transformation intended to serve?
2. Was the purpose framed primarily in terms of delivery, adoption, capability, or value realization?
3. Were multiple purposes acknowledged, and if so, how were they prioritized?
4. How was success expected to be recognized at different points in time?

---

## Governance

1. What governance structures oversaw the transformation initiative?
2. How was governance success defined (e.g., milestones, budget, scope adherence)?
3. Were adoption or value outcomes explicitly governed, or implicitly assumed?
4. What decision rights existed for redefining success post-delivery?
5. How were trade-offs between predictability and responsiveness governed?

---

## Scope

1. How was the scope of the transformation defined?
2. Did scope emphasize deliverables, capabilities, behaviors, or outcomes?
3. What elements were explicitly included or excluded from scope?
4. Were post-delivery adoption activities within scope or out of scope?
5. How rigid or flexible was scope once delivery began?

---

## Work

1. What work was required to deliver the transformation?
2. What additional work was required to adopt or integrate the delivered resolution?
3. Were these work types distinguished or conflated?
4. Who was responsible for adoption-related work?
5. Was adoption work resourced, scheduled, or assumed?

---

## Value

1. What value outcomes were anticipated from the transformation?
2. For whom was value expected to be realized (users, organization, leadership)?
3. How was value expected to emerge over time?
4. Were delivery metrics treated as proxies for value?
5. How were value shortfalls expected to be identified?

---

## Communication

1. How was transformation success communicated during delivery?
2. How were expectations about adoption and use communicated?
3. Were distinctions made between delivery completion and transformation success?
4. How were concerns or dissatisfaction communicated post-delivery?

---

## Collaboration

1. Which stakeholders collaborated during design and delivery?
2. Which stakeholders collaborated during rollout and adoption?
3. Were users involved in shaping post-delivery practices?
4. How were disagreements or tensions surfaced and addressed?
5. Did collaboration continue after formal delivery completion?
