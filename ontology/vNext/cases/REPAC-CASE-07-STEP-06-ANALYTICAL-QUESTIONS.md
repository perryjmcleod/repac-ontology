# REPAC Case 07 — Step 06: Analytical Questions

## Purpose of This Step
This step establishes the analytical questions required to explain the conditions observed in Case 07. The objective is to discipline inquiry across REPAC domains and to prevent reduction of the case to a single explanatory factor such as governance failure, communication breakdown, or individual performance.

## Analytical Questions by Domain

### Purpose
- What organizational purpose(s) were formally associated with the work in this case?
- How did participants interpret or prioritize purpose when facing operational constraints?

### Imperative
- What imperatives (time pressure, risk avoidance, performance expectations, dependency constraints) shaped behavior?
- Were these imperatives explicit, implicit, or emergent during execution?

### Scope
- How was scope formally defined with respect to responsibilities, handoffs, and boundaries?
- Where did actual execution require reinterpretation or expansion of scope?

### Work
- What work was expected to occur according to formal descriptions or plans?
- What work was actually performed, deferred, substituted, or omitted in practice?

### Governance
- What governance mechanisms were intended to guide decision-making and accountability?
- How consistently were these mechanisms applied under real operating conditions?

### Communication
- How were expectations, assumptions, and changes communicated?
- Where did communication rely on inference rather than explicit articulation?

### Collaboration
- Who was expected to collaborate formally, and who collaborated in practice?
- How were cross-boundary dependencies resolved when formal coordination was insufficient?

### Value
- What value outcomes were anticipated by the organization?
- What value was realized, delayed, displaced, or placed at risk as a result of the case conditions?
