# VNEXT-0005 — Pilot Output Policy

## Purpose
Define the authoritative status, constraints, and promotion rules governing all REPAC vNext pilot decompositions.

## Policy Statement
All pilot Subsets and Elements are non-normative, non-authoritative, and exploratory until explicitly promoted through REPAC governance.

Pilot artifacts:
- do not extend REPAC v1.0,
- do not redefine Superset semantics,
- do not imply recommended practices, workflows, or methods.

## Allowed Pilot Outputs
Pilot phases may produce:
- markdown governance artifacts,
- eligibility and cross-check matrices,
- reject logs documenting excluded constructs.

Pilot phases may NOT produce:
- normative ontology extensions,
- prescriptive guidance,
- maturity models,
- procedural representations.

## Promotion Gate (Pilot → Formal Extension)
A pilot construct MAY be promoted only if it satisfies all of the following:
1. Repeated analytical utility across multiple cases.
2. No semantic overlap with other Supersets.
3. No procedural, evaluative, or temporal semantics.
4. Successful eligibility revalidation under VNEXT-0001.
5. Explicit governance approval recorded in-repo.

## Authority Boundary
Until promotion:
- pilot constructs have no binding interpretive authority,
- pilot constructs must not be cited as REPAC definitions,
- pilot constructs must remain isolated from v1.0 formal modules.

## Governance Lock
This policy applies to all future pilot phases unless explicitly superseded by a higher-numbered VNEXT governance artifact.
