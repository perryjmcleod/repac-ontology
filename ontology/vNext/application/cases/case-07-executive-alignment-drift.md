## Problem Statement (Bounded)

An organizational change initiative was launched with a structured communication plan, including clear messaging, multiple communication channels, and documented expectations. Despite this, adoption was low and intended changes in behavior or practice did not materialize.

The issue under analysis is not whether communication occurred.  
The issue is whether communication alone was sufficient to enable coordinated action and sustained change.

---

## Salient Facts (Observable)

1. A formal change initiative was launched.
2. A documented communication plan accompanied the initiative.
3. Information about the change was distributed through multiple channels.
4. Expectations and timelines were communicated to affected stakeholders.
5. Adoption of the change was lower than anticipated.
6. Existing practices continued alongside or instead of the new approach.
7. No widespread claims of missing or unclear communication were raised.
8. The initiative did not achieve its intended level of impact within the observed period.

---

## Explicit Exclusions and Unknowns

- Quality or clarity of individual communications
- Training or enablement efforts beyond communication
- Incentive structures tied to adoption
- Individual resistance motivations
- Long-term adoption beyond the initial period
- Comparative adoption data from similar initiatives

---

## Notes
This case distinguishes **information transfer** from **behavioral coordination**.
