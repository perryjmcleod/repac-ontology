---

## Problem Statement (Bounded)

A decision was made in full compliance with documented organizational policies and approval processes. Despite this compliance, the resulting outcomes were widely perceived as negative by affected stakeholders and led to measurable operational or morale impacts.

The issue under analysis is not whether policies were followed.  
The issue is whether policy compliance alone was sufficient to ensure alignment with intended purpose and value outcomes.

---

## Salient Facts (Observable)

1. A formal decision was made within the organization.
2. The decision followed documented governance and approval procedures.
3. Required sign-offs and authorizations were obtained.
4. The decision resulted in outcomes perceived as negative by one or more stakeholder groups.
5. Operational disruption, reduced morale, or reduced effectiveness was observed following implementation.
6. No formal policy violations were identified.
7. Concerns raised focused on outcomes rather than procedural legitimacy.
8. The decision remained in effect after concerns were expressed.

---

## Explicit Exclusions and Unknowns

- Whether policies were optimally designed
- Whether alternative compliant decisions were considered
- Individual intent of decision-makers
- Long-term outcomes beyond the observed period
- Whether policy revisions were proposed or enacted afterward
- Whether similar outcomes occurred in comparable past decisions

---

## Notes
This case explicitly separates **policy compliance** from **value realization**.
