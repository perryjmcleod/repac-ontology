# SOURCE-L1-SUBSET—documentary

## Source
Migrated from REPAC v1.0 (`repac-source.owl`) and normalized for vNext L1 packaging.

## Definition
A Documentary Source is a source whose authority, constraint, or directional influence is carried primarily through a document, record, or formally captured artefact.

## Scope Boundary
This subset covers sources that function through **documented content** (written, recorded, or otherwise captured) that can be referenced independently of a specific person or system at the moment of influence.

## Included
- Policies, standards, procedures, and guidelines
- Contracts, agreements, terms, and formal commitments
- Records of decision (minutes, approvals, signed directives)
- Regulations and compliance documents as artefacts

## Excluded
- A person acting as the source (covered by **Human Source**)
- Automated enforcement or constraints generated by a system (covered by **System Source**)
- Physical conditions and situational constraints (covered by **Environmental Source**)

## Notes
This is an L1 subset definition only. Documentary classifications and document-structure modelling are deferred to controlled explosion.
