# SOURCE L1 Subset — Environmental Source

## Source
Migrated from REPAC v1.0 (`repac-source.owl`) and normalized for vNext L1 packaging.

## Definition
An Environmental Source is a source whose constraint or directional influence originates in the physical, operational, or situational environment in which work occurs.

## Scope Boundary
This subset covers sources that function through **environmental conditions** rather than human intent, system logic, or documentary authority.

## Included
- Physical constraints (space, location, climate, infrastructure limits)
- Operational constraints (capacity, time windows, availability conditions)
- Situational constraints (emergent conditions affecting feasible work execution)

## Excluded
- Decisions or approvals by people (covered by **Human Source**)
- Rules enforced by applications or automation (covered by **System Source**)
- Mandates carried by documented artefacts (covered by **Documentary Source**)

## Notes
This is an L1 subset definition only. Environmental subtyping and modelling of constraint mechanics are deferred to controlled explosion.
