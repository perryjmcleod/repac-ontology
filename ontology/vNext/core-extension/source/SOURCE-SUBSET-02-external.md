# SOURCE-SUBSET-02-external

## Definition

External Source refers to source arising from outside the defined boundary of the focal entity, system, or unit under analysis.

## Scope

Includes:

- sources arising from outside the focal boundary
- third-party inputs
- environmental or outside-system sources
- source conditions located beyond the scoped system

Excludes:

- sources arising from within the focal boundary
- internally generated inputs
- internal actors, systems, and artefacts as sources
- source conditions located inside the scoped system

## Constraints

- This subset functions at the subset level only
- No element layer exists within this subset
- No decomposition into external source types is permitted

## Integrity Check

- The subset is distinct from Internal Source
- The subset is boundary-dependent and must be interpreted relative to Scope
- The subset does not imply composition, structure, or elementhood
- The subset remains aligned with authoritative cross-superset boundary rules

## Boundary Enforcement (Authoritative)

This subset exists within a subset-terminal, non-element-bearing superset.

External Source must not be interpreted as an element-bearing or structurally decomposable construct.

External Source must be interpreted relative to the defined analytical boundary, not as an absolute category.
