# SOURCE-L1-SUBSET—human

## Source
Migrated from REPAC v1.0 (`repac-source.owl`) and normalized for vNext L1 packaging.

## Definition
A Human Source is a source whose authority, intent, constraint, or directional influence originates in a person or group of people acting in a human capacity.

## Scope Boundary
This subset covers sources that exist as **human-originating agents** (individuals or collectives) whose decisions, approvals, requests, expectations, or judgments function as a source within analysis.

## Included
- An individual decision-maker or approver
- A team, committee, or stakeholder group acting as a collective human agent
- A human expert providing interpretive or judgment-based input

## Excluded
- Automated or computational sources (covered by **System Source**)
- Static artefacts as primary sources (covered by **Documentary Source**)
- Physical or situational constraints not originating in a person (covered by **Environmental Source**)

## Notes
This is an L1 subset definition only. Elements, roles, and finer distinctions are deferred to controlled explosion.
