# COMM-SUBSET-01-formal

## Definition

Formal communication consists of structured, intentional, and explicitly governed exchanges of information within an organizational or operational context.

## Scope

Includes:

- Official channels
- Documented communication
- Policy-driven exchanges
- Scheduled and sanctioned interactions

Excludes:

- Spontaneous or unofficial interactions
- Informal or ad hoc exchanges

## Constraints

- No element layer exists within this subset
- No decomposition beyond the subset level is permitted

## Integrity Check

- The subset is distinct from informal communication
- The subset is not decomposed into communication units or elements

## Boundary Enforcement (Authoritative)

This subset exists within a subset-terminal, non-element-bearing superset.

No element-level constructs are permitted.
