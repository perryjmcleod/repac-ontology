# COMMUNICATION — Definition

## Definition

Communication defines the transmission or exchange of information, signals, or meaning between actors. Communication enables awareness and understanding but does not, on its own, constitute coordinated action.

## Clarification

Communication concerns the movement and interpretation of information. It enables actors to become informed but does not ensure alignment in action.

## Boundary Statement

Communication is distinct from Collaboration.

Communication governs information exchange.  
Collaboration governs alignment in action.

Communication enables awareness.  
Collaboration requires coordinated activity.

## Critical Note

Information exchange alone does not constitute collaboration. Communication may support collaboration but does not establish it.

## Prohibited Constructions

The following constructions are not valid within the REPAC ontology:

- communication is collaboration  
- collaboration equals communication  
- meetings are collaboration without coordinated action  
- information sharing equals collaboration
