# COMM-SUBSET-02-informal

## Definition

Informal communication consists of unstructured, spontaneous, and non-governed exchanges of information arising from social, contextual, or emergent interactions.

## Scope

Includes:

- Casual conversations
- Unscheduled interactions
- Social exchanges
- Emergent coordination dialogue

Excludes:

- Policy-driven communication
- Official or documented exchanges

## Constraints

- No element layer exists within this subset
- No decomposition beyond the subset level is permitted

## Integrity Check

- The subset is distinct from formal communication
- The subset is not decomposed into communication units or elements

## Boundary Enforcement (Authoritative)

This subset exists within a subset-terminal, non-element-bearing superset.

No element-level constructs are permitted.
