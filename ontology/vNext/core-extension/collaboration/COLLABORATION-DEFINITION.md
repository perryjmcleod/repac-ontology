# COLLABORATION — Definition

## Definition

Collaboration defines the coordinated or emergent alignment of multiple actors engaged in shared or interacting activity. Collaboration requires alignment in action and cannot be reduced to information exchange alone.

## Clarification

Collaboration concerns how actors work together. It may be structured or emergent but always involves alignment in activity.

Collaboration requires more than communication.

## Boundary Statement

Collaboration is distinct from Communication.

Collaboration governs coordinated action.  
Communication governs information exchange.

Collaboration requires alignment.  
Communication enables awareness.

## Critical Note

Communication may support collaboration, but it does not constitute it. Alignment in action is required for collaboration to exist.

## Prohibited Constructions

The following constructions are not valid within the REPAC ontology:

- collaboration equals communication  
- communication is collaboration  
- information exchange alone is collaboration
