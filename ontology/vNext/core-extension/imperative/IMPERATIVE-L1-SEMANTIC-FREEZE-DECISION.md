# IMPERATIVE L1 — Semantic Freeze Decision

## Objective

Lock the semantic interpretation of the Imperative superset.

## Constraints

- No reinterpretation after freeze

## Frozen Subsets

1. `IMP-SUBSET-01-mandatory`
2. `IMP-SUBSET-02-discretionary`

## Frozen Semantics

- Imperative identifies the degree of obligation associated with a phenomenon
- Mandatory refers to conditions where the phenomenon is required or non-optional
- Discretionary refers to conditions where the phenomenon is optional or subject to choice
- Imperative does not define execution, action units, or decision structures

## Integrity Check

- Semantic boundaries are clear and stable
- No residual drift into behavioural modelling exists
- Imperative remains subset-terminal and non-element-bearing

## Boundary Enforcement (Authoritative)
