# WORK L1 Subset — Outcome Work

## Source
Defined in REPAC vNext. Work module in REPAC v1.0 is an unpopulated stub; no subset structure is available to migrate.

## Definition
Outcome Work is work understood in terms of what it produces: an output, deliverable, result, or effect.

## Scope Boundary
This subset covers work analysed by its resulting product or change, where the defining characteristic is the achieved outcome rather than the tasks or process used.

## Included
- A deliverable or produced artefact as the focal unit of analysis
- A measurable result or effect (including service outcomes)
- Outcome-oriented work framing used for evaluation or accountability

## Excluded
- Execution steps analysed as units (covered by **Task Work**)
- Work analysed primarily as repeatable flow (covered by **Process Work**)

## Notes
This is an L1 subset definition only. Outcome categories and evaluation mechanics are deferred to controlled explosion.
