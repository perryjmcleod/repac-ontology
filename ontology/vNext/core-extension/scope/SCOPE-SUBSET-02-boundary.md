# SCOPE-SUBSET-02-Boundary

## Definition
Boundary is the structural partition that defines what is inside versus outside the asserted scope, given a selected Perspective, Granularity, and Interval.

## Role in Scope L1
Boundary delimits the analytical universe by making inclusion and exclusion explicit as outcomes of partition.

## Analytical Tests
- What entities are treated as in-scope?
- What entities are treated as out-of-scope?
- If exclusion cannot be stated, what information is missing to make Boundary coherent?

## Non-Equivalences
Boundary is not:
- governance control, ownership, accountability, or authority
- an enforcement mechanism
- a subtype system at L1 (for example, “organizational boundary”); such distinctions are determined by Perspective

## Notes
Inclusion and Exclusion are outcomes (properties) of Boundary assertion and are not independent Scope L1 Subsets.
