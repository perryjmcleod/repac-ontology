# SCOPE L1 — Candidate Subsets

## Objective
Identify the controlled Layer 1 candidate subsets for Scope in REPAC vNext using the stabilized reclassification rule already established during vNext.

## Candidate Scope L1 Subsets

1. `SCOPE-SUBSET-01-internal`
2. `SCOPE-SUBSET-02-external`

## Rationale

During the vNext stabilization process, Internal Source and External Source were permanently reclassified as Scope-related constructs rather than Source subsets.

This establishes Scope as the proper location for internal and external boundary distinctions.

The candidate subset structure therefore classifies Scope by bounded locus of relevance:

- **Internal** — within the boundary of the focal entity, system, or frame
- **External** — outside the boundary of the focal entity, system, or frame

This distinction preserves the separation between:
- Scope and Source
- Boundary and origin
- Relevance and evidence

## Constraints

- No conceptual expansion
- No ontology drift
- No OWL/TTL population
- Apply the permanent reclassification rule consistently
