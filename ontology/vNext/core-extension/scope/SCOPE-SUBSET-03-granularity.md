# SCOPE-SUBSET-03-granularity

## Definition

Granularity defines the level of detail or resolution at which a phenomenon is examined.

## Scope

Includes:

- level of detail
- resolution of analysis
- degree of refinement
- scale of observation

Excludes:

- structural decomposition into elements (Work)
- classification of origin (Source)

## Constraints

- This subset functions at the subset level only
- No element layer exists within this subset
- No structural decomposition is permitted

## Integrity Check

- The subset is distinct from Perspective, Boundary, and Interval
- The subset does not imply composition or elementhood
- The subset remains aligned with authoritative cross-superset boundary rules

## Boundary Enforcement (Authoritative)

This subset exists within a subset-terminal, non-element-bearing superset.

Granularity must not be interpreted as a structural or element-bearing construct.
