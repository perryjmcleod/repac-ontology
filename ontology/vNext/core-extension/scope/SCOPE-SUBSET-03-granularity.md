# SCOPE-SUBSET-03-Granularity

## Definition
Granularity is the resolution at which in-scope entities are treated and analyzed. It specifies the level of analytical detail applied to entities admitted by Boundary under the selected Perspective.

## Role in Scope L1
Granularity changes how included entities are examined without altering what is included.

## Analytical Tests
- At what resolution is the explanation being made (coarse vs. fine)?
- Is disagreement caused by resolution mismatch rather than factual contradiction?

## Non-Equivalences
Granularity is not:
- organizational rank, authority level, or hierarchy as a separate construct
- a decomposition of execution steps, a work breakdown, or a project plan
