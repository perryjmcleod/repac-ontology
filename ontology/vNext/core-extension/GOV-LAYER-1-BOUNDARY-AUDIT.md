# Governance Superset — Layer 1 Boundary Audit
VNEXT Phase: Controlled Superset Explosion

## Purpose of This Document

This artifact records the boundary discipline applied to Governance Layer 1 elements following cross-case stress testing. Its purpose is to prevent cross-superset leakage, semantic drift, and prescriptive collapse.

Governance remains necessary but not sufficient for explanation.

---

# Boundary Audit by Adjacent Superset

## 1. Governance vs Scope

### Risk
Escalation Logic and Authority Structure may drift into boundary definition of Scope.

### Clarification
Scope defines what is inside or outside analytical consideration.  
Governance defines who controls decisions within that Scope.

### Locked Boundary
Governance does not define system boundaries.  
Scope does not define power distribution.

---

## 2. Governance vs Work

### Risk
Decision Rights Allocation may collapse into task ownership.  
Oversight Construct may collapse into supervision.

### Clarification
Work concerns execution activity.  
Governance concerns structural entitlement and evaluative control.

### Locked Boundary
Governance does not describe how work is performed.  
Work does not define structural authority legitimacy.

---

## 3. Governance vs Communication

### Risk
Escalation Logic may be misinterpreted as communication breakdown.

### Clarification
Escalation Logic defines formal elevation pathways.  
Communication concerns information transmission quality and clarity.

### Locked Boundary
Governance escalation failure is structural absence of elevation mechanism.  
Communication failure is distortion or breakdown of information exchange.

---

## 4. Governance vs Collaboration

### Risk
Informal Governance Overlay may be confused with collaborative dynamics.

### Clarification
Collaboration concerns coordinated interaction patterns.  
Informal Governance Overlay concerns patterned power influence structures.

### Locked Boundary
Collaboration may operate within informal governance, but it does not define structural authority legitimacy.

---

## 5. Governance vs Value

### Risk
Oversight Construct may appear value-defining.

### Clarification
Value defines outcome assessment constructs.  
Oversight monitors governance performance relative to defined Value but does not define Value itself.

### Locked Boundary
Governance evaluates adherence to authority constructs.  
Value evaluates outcome significance.

# Internal Element Distinction Audit

## Enforcement Mechanism vs Compliance Mechanism

Enforcement = consequence structure.  
Compliance = measurement and traceability structure.

They are structurally distinct and analytically separable.

---

## Authority Structure vs Decision Rights Allocation

Authority Structure = structural positioning of power.  
Decision Rights Allocation = entitlement assignment to decision categories.

One concerns structure; the other concerns entitlement mapping.

# Revision Triggers

Governance Layer 1 must be revised if:

- Elements repeatedly collapse into other Supersets during case analysis
- Two or more elements cannot be analytically distinguished in real cases
- Formalization requires forced artificial distinctions
- Layer 1 becomes prescriptive rather than analytical

Governance Layer 1 remains structurally distinct, non-prescriptive, and portable across cases.
