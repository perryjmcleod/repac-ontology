# PUR-SUBSET-02-declarative

## Definition

Declarative Purpose refers to purpose expressed, stated, or designated in relation to a phenomenon.

## Scope

Includes:

- stated purpose
- documented purpose
- explicitly expressed intent
- purpose designated through description, policy, or articulation

Excludes:

- purpose arising from the phenomenon itself
- internal intendedness not dependent on declaration

## Constraints

- This subset functions at the subset level only
- No element layer exists within this subset
- No decomposition into goals, actions, or execution is permitted

## Integrity Check

- The subset is distinct from Intentional Purpose
- The subset does not imply composition, structure, or elementhood
- The subset remains aligned with authoritative cross-superset boundary rules

## Boundary Enforcement (Authoritative)

This subset exists within a subset-terminal, non-element-bearing superset.

Declarative Purpose must not be interpreted as an element-bearing or structurally decomposable construct.

## Outcome

STABLE
