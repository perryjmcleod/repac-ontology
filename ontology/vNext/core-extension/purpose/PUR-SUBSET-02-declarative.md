# PUR-SUBSET-02-declarative

## Label

Declarative

## Parent

Purpose

## Level

L1 Subset

## Definition

Declarative purpose captures purpose as stated, expressed, announced, documented, or otherwise made explicit in communicable form.

## Boundary

This subset concerns the declared or externalized articulation of purpose.

It does not by itself guarantee that the declared purpose reflects actual underlying intent.

## Notes

This subset is analytically distinct from intentional purpose. Declared purpose may align with, distort, or conceal intended purpose.
