# PUR-SUBSET-01-intentional

## Definition

Intentional Purpose refers to purpose that is intrinsic to the phenomenon itself rather than externally assigned or articulated.

## Scope

Includes:

- inherent intendedness
- purpose intrinsic to the phenomenon
- purpose arising from the nature or design of the phenomenon itself

Excludes:

- purpose assigned or declared externally
- purpose articulated through explicit statements or external designation

## Constraints

- This subset functions at the subset level only
- No element layer exists within this subset
- No decomposition into goals, actions, or intentions is permitted

## Integrity Check

- The subset is distinct from Declarative Purpose
- The subset does not imply composition, structure, or elementhood
- The subset remains aligned with authoritative cross-superset boundary rules

## Boundary Enforcement (Authoritative)

This subset exists within a subset-terminal, non-element-bearing superset.

Intentional Purpose must not be interpreted as an element-bearing or structurally decomposable construct.

## Outcome

STABLE
