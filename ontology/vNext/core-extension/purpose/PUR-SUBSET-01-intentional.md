# PUR-SUBSET-01-intentional

## Label

Intentional

## Parent

Purpose

## Level

L1 Subset

## Definition

Intentional purpose captures the purposive intent behind an action, decision, structure, or initiative. It refers to the intended reason for doing, organizing, initiating, or directing something.

## Boundary

This subset concerns intended purpose as held, asserted, or driving action.

It does not by itself capture purpose as formally stated, documented, or declared in externalized form.

## Notes

This subset is analytically distinct from declarative purpose. A purpose may be intended without being formally declared.
