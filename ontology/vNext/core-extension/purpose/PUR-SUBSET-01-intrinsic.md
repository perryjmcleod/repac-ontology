# PUR-SUBSET-01 — intrinsic

## Subset ID
`PUR-SUBSET-01-intrinsic`

## Label
Intrinsic

## Definition
A purpose that arises from the inherent nature, structure, or behavior of an entity or system.

## Description
Represents purpose that exists independent of external assignment. Intrinsic purpose is derived from what the entity or system does by its nature or design.

Purpose defines why something exists. Intrinsic purpose reflects internally arising direction or function, without requiring external designation.

## Constraints
- Must arise from the nature or structure of the entity or system
- Does not require external assignment or declaration
- Does not encode value judgment or outcome

## Usage Examples

- A thermostat regulates temperature within a system
- A learning process leads to knowledge acquisition through engagement
- A system stabilizes its internal state during operation
