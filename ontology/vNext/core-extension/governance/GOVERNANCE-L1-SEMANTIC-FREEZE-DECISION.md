# GOVERNANCE L1 — Semantic Freeze Decision

## Objective

Lock the semantic interpretation of the Governance superset.

## Constraints

- No reinterpretation after freeze
- Element layer must remain controlled
- No cross-superset semantic leakage
- Frozen semantics are authoritative

## Frozen Subsets

1. `GOV-SUBSET-01-authority`
2. `GOV-SUBSET-02-decision-rights`
3. `GOV-SUBSET-03-control`
4. `GOV-SUBSET-04-accountability`

## Frozen Semantics

- Governance defines the structures and conditions through which legitimate direction, decision allocation, control, and responsibility are established and maintained
- Authority defines the basis and structure through which governance legitimacy and standing are constituted
- Decision Rights defines how decisional power is allocated and escalated
- Control defines how governance conditions are enforced, monitored, and maintained
- Accountability defines how answerability, oversight, and responsibility are established and assessed
- Governance is a valid carrier of element-level constructs

## Integrity Check

- Semantic boundaries are clear and stable
- No overlap exists with Work, Communication, or Collaboration
- Element layer is justified and contained

## Boundary Enforcement (Authoritative)

Governance is an element-bearing superset.

Governance contains governance-specific element constructs and must not be reduced to communication, collaboration, or execution.
