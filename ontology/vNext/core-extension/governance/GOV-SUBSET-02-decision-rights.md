# GOV-SUBSET-02-decision-rights

## Definition

Decision Rights defines how decisional authority is allocated, distributed, limited, and escalated within a governance structure.

## Scope

Includes:

- allocation of decisional authority
- decision ownership
- escalation paths
- authorized decisional boundaries

Excludes:

- execution of decisions
- communication acts used to convey decisions
- accountability assessment after decision execution

## Constraints

- This subset supports element placement
- No structural decomposition is permitted beyond the authorized element layer
- Decision Rights must remain distinct from Work execution

## Integrity Check

- The subset is distinct from Authority, Control, and Accountability
- The subset does not collapse into execution or communication
- The subset remains aligned with authoritative cross-superset boundary rules

## Boundary Enforcement (Authoritative)

This subset exists within an element-bearing superset.

Decision Rights elements must remain governance-specific and must not be reclassified as Work or Communication constructs.

STABLE
