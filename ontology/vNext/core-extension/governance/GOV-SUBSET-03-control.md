# GOV-SUBSET-03-control

## Definition

Control defines the mechanisms through which governance conditions are enforced, monitored, stabilized, and maintained.

## Scope

Includes:

- enforcement conditions
- compliance conditions
- governance monitoring mechanisms
- stabilizing governance interventions

Excludes:

- authority standing itself
- decision allocation itself
- accountability judgment as such

## Constraints

- This subset supports element placement
- No structural decomposition is permitted beyond the authorized element layer
- Control must remain distinct from operational work procedures

## Integrity Check

- The subset is distinct from Authority, Decision Rights, and Accountability
- The subset does not collapse into Work or Communication
- The subset remains aligned with authoritative cross-superset boundary rules

## Boundary Enforcement (Authoritative)

This subset exists within an element-bearing superset.

Control elements must remain governance-specific and must not be reclassified as execution, communication, or collaboration constructs.

## Outcome

STABLE
