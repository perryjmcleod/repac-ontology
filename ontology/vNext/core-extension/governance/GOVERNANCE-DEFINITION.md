# GOVERNANCE — Definition

## Definition

Governance defines the structural conditions that regulate decision-making, authority, control, and accountability. Governance establishes the conditions under which work is permitted, constrained, or evaluated and does not itself execute work.

## Clarification

Governance determines who can decide, how decisions are legitimized, how compliance is enforced, and how accountability is assigned.

Governance is structural. It is not execution.

## Boundary Statement

Governance is distinct from Work.

Governance defines decision structures and control conditions.  
Work performs actions and produces outcomes.

Governance establishes conditions for action.  
Work executes within or against those conditions.

## Critical Note

Governance may be embedded within work execution, but embedding does not change ontological classification. Governance remains structural even when operationalized.

## Prohibited Constructions

The following constructions are not valid within the REPAC ontology:

- governance activities when referring to execution steps  
- process equals governance  
- execution is governance  
- work defines authority
