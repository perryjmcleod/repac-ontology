# Governance Superset — Layer 1 Internal Relationship Hypotheses
VNEXT Phase G3 — Structural Topology Modelling

## Purpose

This artifact proposes structural relationship hypotheses among Governance Layer 1 elements.

These are analytical dependency statements, not workflows, not causal prescriptions, and not process models.

They articulate:
- Structural precedence
- Structural dependency
- Structural conditioning
- Structural orthogonality

All relationships remain non-prescriptive and subject to revision under formalization.

---

# Relationship Types Used

- Structural Precondition: Element A must exist for Element B to function coherently.
- Structural Conditioning: Element A influences the stability or effectiveness of Element B.
- Structural Independence: Elements can exist without logical dependency.
- Structural Tension: Elements may conflict under certain configurations.

---

# Core Structural Relationships

## 1. Authority Structure ↔ Decision Rights Allocation

### Hypothesis
Authority Structure is a structural precondition for coherent Decision Rights Allocation.

### Rationale
Decision entitlement requires recognized positional power context.

### Boundary
Authority can exist without explicit entitlement mapping.
Entitlement mapping cannot exist coherently without some authority structure.

---

## 2. Authority Structure ↔ Legitimacy Basis

### Hypothesis
Legitimacy Basis conditions the stability of Authority Structure.

### Rationale
Authority without legitimacy produces formal recognition without functional compliance.

### Classification
Structural Conditioning (not strict dependency).

---

## 3. Decision Rights Allocation ↔ Enforcement Mechanism

### Hypothesis
Enforcement Mechanism presupposes defined Decision Rights Allocation.

### Rationale
Consequence structures require clarity about who was entitled to decide.

### Classification
Structural Precondition.

---

## 4. Enforcement Mechanism ↔ Compliance Mechanism

### Hypothesis
Compliance Mechanism is analytically independent from Enforcement Mechanism but often operationally linked.

### Rationale
Measurement of adherence can exist without consequence.
Consequence can theoretically exist without measurement tracking.

### Classification
Structural Independence with common coupling.

---

## 5. Escalation Logic ↔ Authority Structure

### Hypothesis
Escalation Logic presupposes hierarchical or differentiated Authority Structure.

### Rationale
Elevation pathways require structurally recognized power differentials.

### Classification
Structural Precondition.

---

## 6. Escalation Logic ↔ Decision Rights Allocation

### Hypothesis
Escalation Logic activates when Decision Rights Allocation boundaries are exceeded or contested.

### Classification
Structural Conditioning.

---

## 7. Oversight Construct ↔ Authority Structure

### Hypothesis
Oversight Construct requires separation from operational Authority Structure to function coherently.

### Rationale
Self-monitoring authority collapses evaluative independence.

### Classification
Structural Tension if separation absent.

---

## 8. Oversight Construct ↔ Compliance Mechanism

### Hypothesis
Oversight may depend on Compliance Mechanism for visibility but is not reducible to it.

### Classification
Conditional Dependency.

---

## 9. Informal Governance Overlay ↔ Formal Authority Structure

### Hypothesis
Informal Governance Overlay emerges when Legitimacy Basis diverges from formal Authority Structure.

### Classification
Structural Tension / Emergent Overlay.

---

## 10. Informal Governance Overlay ↔ Enforcement Mechanism

### Hypothesis
Weak or inconsistent Enforcement Mechanism increases probability of Informal Governance Overlay stabilization.

### Classification
Structural Conditioning.

---

# Non-Relationships (Explicitly Rejected)

To preserve boundary discipline:

- Governance elements do not imply Value definition.
- Governance elements do not define Scope boundaries.
- No element implies temporal sequencing.
- No element implies best practice configuration.

---

# Structural Integrity Summary

Governance Layer 1 exhibits:

- Clear precondition chains
- Conditional stabilization relationships
- Independent but interacting constructs
- No forced circular dependency

Topology remains acyclic at structural precondition level.

# Formalization Note

Before OWL modelling:

- Precondition relationships may translate into object properties (e.g., requires, conditions, stabilizes).
- Structural Independence must not be encoded as mandatory linkage.
- Structural Tension may require modelling as incompatible or destabilizing states.

No formal axioms are committed at this stage.
