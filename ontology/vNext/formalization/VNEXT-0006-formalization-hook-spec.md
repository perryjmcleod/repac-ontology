# VNEXT-0006 — Formalization Hook Specification (Design Only)

## Purpose
Define a deterministic, non-authoritative formalization hook for future pilot promotion without committing to ontology extension.

This specification is inert unless explicitly activated by governance.

## Scope
This document defines:
- what MAY be formalized later,
- how it must be structurally represented,
- and what is explicitly prohibited.

## Formalization Eligibility
Only pilot constructs that have:
- passed VNEXT-0007 evaluation, and
- been approved under VNEXT-0005
may be considered for formalization.

## Permitted Formal Content (If Activated)
If formalization is approved in a future phase, it must be limited to:
- stable identifiers,
- labels,
- textual definitions,
- explicit parent relationships (Superset → Subset → Element).

No axioms beyond structural containment are permitted at this stage.

## Explicit Prohibitions
Formalization must NOT introduce:
- process logic,
- sequencing or stages,
- normative constraints,
- competency or capability claims,
- evaluation or quality assertions.

## Namespace Discipline
Any future formalization must:
- reside in a vNext namespace or module,
- remain isolated from v1.0 authoritative artifacts,
- be removable without semantic impact to REPAC v1.0.

## Status
This document is a design constraint, not an instruction to formalize.
