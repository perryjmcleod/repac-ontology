# Governance — Core Claims (Pilot)

1. Governance models decision rights, accountability structures, and control constraints that shape what actions are permissible, expected, or blocked.
2. Governance is necessary but not sufficient for explanation: it constrains Work and interacts with Scope, but does not determine outcomes alone.
3. Governance breakdown is analysable as mismatch among stated decision rights, enacted decision rights, and evidenced accountability and control.
4. Governance interacts with Scope through boundary authority: who can set, revise, or waive inclusions, exclusions, assumptions, and deferrals.
5. Governance explanations require explicit evidence artifacts (policies, delegations, approvals, audit trails, exception records) rather than post-hoc attribution.
