# Version and Citation Statement: REPAC Ontology v1.0

This document defines the version scope, immutability guarantees, and citation guidance for the REPAC Ontology v1.0.

---

## Version Status

REPAC Ontology v1.0 is a stable, publication-ready release.

The ontology is complete and frozen at the Superset and Subset levels. All Supersets, Subsets, their boundaries, and their formal relationships are considered authoritative for this version.

---

## Immutability Guarantees

For REPAC v1.0:

- No Supersets will be added, removed, renamed, or redefined
- No Subsets will be added, removed, renamed, or redefined
- No boundary rules between Supersets or Subsets will be altered
- No dependency or constraint relationships will be reinterpreted

Any future work will occur through explicitly versioned releases and will not retroactively modify v1.0 semantics.

---

## Permitted Non-Semantic Changes

The following changes may occur without affecting semantic stability:

- Documentation clarifications
- Typographical corrections
- Non-normative explanatory material
- Repository packaging or tooling improvements

Such changes do not alter the meaning, scope, or evaluative basis of the ontology.

---

## Citation Guidance

REPAC Ontology v1.0 should be cited as a stable analytical artifact.

Citations should reference:

- The ontology name and version (REPAC Ontology v1.0)
- The accompanying publication
- The public repository containing the formal ontology artifacts

Where differences in wording occur, formal ontology files and canonical diagrams take precedence over narrative descriptions.

---

## Evaluation Boundary

If a requested change requires the addition, removal, or reinterpretation of a Superset, Subset, or boundary rule, it is outside the scope of REPAC Ontology v1.0.
