# Reviewer Positioning and Interpretive Stance

This document records interpretive positioning for the REPAC Ontology v1.0.
It is non-normative and does not modify, extend, or constrain the ontology itself.

Its purpose is to clarify how REPAC should be read, evaluated, and reviewed
by academic and practitioner audiences, particularly in applied ontology
and analytical methods contexts.

## Scope and Non-Goals

This document does not:
- Add new constructs, Supersets, or rules
- Defend REPAC against all philosophical disagreement
- Prescribe how REPAC must be adopted or implemented
- Replace formal ontology artifacts or governance rules

It exists solely to clarify analytical intent and evaluation criteria.

## Ontological Posture

REPAC is an applied analytical ontology.

It defines analytically necessary domains for organizational reasoning and
enforces non-substitutability between those domains. Its primary contribution
is not representational completeness but explanatory constraint.

REPAC is concerned with:
- What forms of explanation are valid
- What forms of explanation are invalid
- How category collapse and single-cause attribution are prevented

REPAC does not attempt to model organizations exhaustively, nor does it claim
that all organizational phenomena can be fully specified.

## Boundary Discipline

REPAC Supersets are not heuristic dimensions or thematic categories.
They are analytically necessary domains whose substitution invalidates analysis.

Boundary enforcement rules function as ontological constraints, not
methodological advice. Certain classifications are explicitly prohibited,
regardless of analyst preference or narrative convenience.

Disagreement with these boundaries is a philosophical position, not evidence
of analytical weakness.

## Non-Reductionism

REPAC explicitly rejects:
- Single-domain causation
- Root cause analysis
- Retrospective narrative convergence

Each Superset is treated as necessary but not sufficient. Failure and success
are understood as emergent states produced by interacting constraints.

REPAC’s non-reductionism is structural rather than rhetorical.

## Role of Demonstrations

Worked examples in the repository demonstrate how REPAC constrains reasoning.
They do not exhaustively prove all possible invalid explanations.

REPAC prevents invalid explanation by disallowing entire classes of reasoning,
not by enumerating every incorrect narrative.

## Evaluation Guidance for Reviewers

REPAC should be evaluated on whether it:
- Enforces analytical boundaries consistently
- Prevents category collapse under pressure
- Disallows substitution of one domain for another
- Maintains non-reductionism in practice

REPAC should not be evaluated based on:
- Whether it provides recommendations
- Whether it computes outcomes
- Whether it aligns with specific management doctrines
- Whether it resolves all ambiguity in organizational life

REPAC constrains analysis. It does not simplify organizations.

## Versioning Note

This positioning applies to REPAC Ontology v1.0.
Future versions may expand formalization or tooling, but no such expansion
is implied here.
