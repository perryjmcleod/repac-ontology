# Reviewer Positioning: REPAC Ontology v1.0

This document clarifies how the REPAC Ontology v1.0 should be positioned and evaluated within applied ontology and organizational analysis research.

It is intended to support fair, well-scoped review.

---

## Nature of the Contribution

REPAC v1.0 is a structural and analytical contribution that supports disciplined explanation of how intentional systems reason from purpose to value under constraint.

It introduces a formally defined set of analytical dimensions and boundaries intended to make reasoning about organizational work explicit, traceable, and evaluable. REPAC is not an execution model, management method, or decision procedure.

---

## Contribution Characteristics

REPAC v1.0 is characterized by:

- Explicit analytical Supersets representing necessary dimensions of reasoning
- Subset structures that define internal scope without operationalization
- Enforced boundary rules that prevent semantic collapse between concerns
- Directional structural relationships expressed as dependency and constraint
- Formal representation using OWL, with governance constraints expressed through SHACL

REPAC does not introduce new organizational concepts. Its contribution lies in how familiar constructs are separated, constrained, and related within a formal analytical scheme.

---

## Scope of Causality

REPAC encodes causal direction in a structural and analytical sense.

Purpose motivates Imperatives. Imperatives constrain Scope. Scope bounds Work. Work produces outcomes evaluated as Value. Governance authorizes and constrains decisions across these relationships.

This causal direction expresses dependency and constraint, not behavioral execution logic, empirical causation, or predictive mechanism.

REPAC does not encode behavioral execution logic, empirical causation, metrics, or prescriptions.

---

## Version Discipline

REPAC v1.0 is complete at the Superset and Subset levels.

Element-level modeling, domain extensions, and applied instantiations are intentionally deferred. No Superset or Subset changes are planned for v1.x.

This discipline preserves analytical stability and comparability across applications.

---

## Relationship to Existing Work

REPAC is complementary to existing frameworks, methods, and modeling approaches.

It is intended to support analysis, comparison, and critique of such approaches without replacing them. REPAC makes no claims regarding superiority, adoption, or effectiveness.

---

## Evaluation Guidance

A fair evaluation of REPAC v1.0 considers:

- Structural clarity and coherence
- Explicitness and enforcement of analytical boundaries
- Internal consistency across diagrams, OWL artifacts, and governance rules
- Alignment between stated intent and formal representation

A fair evaluation does not consider:

- Practical adoption challenges
- Empirical performance validation
- Implementation cost or tooling
- User training or usability

These considerations are outside the scope of v1.0.

---

## Closing Guidance

REPAC v1.0 should be reviewed as a foundational analytical ontology.

Its value lies in the discipline it enforces on reasoning, not in the solutions it prescribes.
